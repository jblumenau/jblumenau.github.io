        <div class="bio">
          <p>My research is focused on how policymakers use evidence to inform their decisions, and on the processes by which research is incorporated into governmental decision-making. I lead the <a href="projects#argie">ARGIE</a> project, funded by a UKRI Metascience Research Grant, which examines the reliability of government impact evaluations. I am part of the <a href="projects#evex">Evidence Exchange (EvEx)</a> consortium, a national network connecting civil and public servants with university researchers.</p>

          <p>I also study what voters want, how politicians act, and how these preferences and behaviours interact to shape electoral outcomes and political representation. My work sits at the intersection of policy evaluation, legislative politics, public opinion, and quantitative methods.</p>

          <p>At the Cabinet Office, I advise on the design and implementation of impact evaluations across government departments, working to strengthen the evidence base for public spending decisions.</p>
